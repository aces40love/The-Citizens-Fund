## Naming and mission

The supplied page calls the initiative **The Citizens Fund**. Its page title and URL use Citizens Bank Foundation. The mission centers on expanding opportunity and strengthening Nashville by investing in its people, projects and partnerships. The four published focus areas are Affordable Housing, Financial Literacy, Workforce Readiness and Community Development.

The source describes housing development and preservation; practical financial education; and stronger neighborhood infrastructure, including community centers, health clinics and small business revitalization. Its Workforce Readiness description incorrectly duplicates the housing description. Any replacement workforce language is editorial copy for review, not a verified description of a formal program.

## Inquiry behavior

The original page uses a four-step organization inquiry. The local redesign preserves its information categories and published choices. Required organization/contact information includes organization name, organization type, contact name, role, email and phone. The other required sections cover focus areas, organization description, people served, project summary, support type, funding range, impact, geographic area and project stage. Website, anticipated timeline and additional context are optional. Choosing Other for organization or support reveals a required explanation field.

Published organization types: Nonprofit; Developer / Housing Partner; Community Organization; Educational Institution; Other. Published support options: Grant funding; Partnership; Capital stack participation; Other. Funding ranges: less than $25,000; $25,000–$100,000; $100,000–$500,000; $500,000+. Stages: Early concept; Planning; Ready to launch; In progress.

The redesigned inquiry is a local preparation tool, with validation, review and an explicit text download. It does not send information, save data in localStorage, or claim to submit an application. Its final link opens the existing official page at its form. Typed answers remain in memory while the page stays open, including when moving backward or closing and reopening the dialog; refreshing discards them. The official form does not receive these answers automatically. A production backend and its operating workflow must be separately configured before this site can accept submissions.

## Facts that must not be invented

The supplied page publishes no donation link, Fund-specific phone/email, leadership list, grant award totals, outcome statistics, award deadlines, turnaround promises, eligibility rules or Fund tax status. It does not verify when the Fund was founded. Do not add an unsupported Donate button, tax-deductibility claim, nonprofit status, or guaranteed funding language.

The bank's official history supports its 1904 founding and CDFI status. These belong to the bank, not necessarily the Fund. The bank's Operation HOPE and The Housing Fund relationships likewise must not be presented as verified Fund projects. Generic bank headquarters contact: (615) 327-9787; 1917 Heiman Street, Nashville, TN 37208. These are not published as dedicated Fund contacts. Citizensbank.com is a different institution and must not be used as the source for this project.
